Teacher notes

Learning goal

Children can say: the Sun stays still, the Earth turns, the side facing the Sun has day and the other side has night.

Key words

Sun, Earth, turn, day, night, bright, dark.

Before the screen (5 minutes)

  1. Darken the room. Hold a torch still: "This is the Sun."
  2. Put a sticker on a ball: "This is the Earth. The sticker is us."
  3. Turn the ball slowly. Ask: "Is our sticker in the light or in the dark?"

On the screen (10 minutes)

  1. Adventure: nine small jobs, each worth a star. Children tap Mimi the cat, Sam the bear and the Sun, turn the Earth to put Mimi to bed and wake her up, and answer two picture questions. Let a different child do each job.
  2. Free play: push the Earth and let go to spin it, count the days, and tap the friends to hear what they say.

After the screen (5 minutes)

Each child is the Earth. Stand facing a lamp: "Day!" Turn slowly on the spot until the lamp is behind you: "Night!" Keep turning: "Good morning!"

What is simplified

  • We look down on the Earth from above the North Pole, so it turns anticlockwise.
  • The real Earth takes one whole day, 24 hours, to turn once.
  • The sizes, the distance and the shapes of the land are not real.
  • The Moon stays in one place here. The real Moon goes slowly around the Earth, about once a month, so its place in the sky changes from day to day.
